OCTIVE: a free VSRG rhythm game

Notes scroll down the lanes and you hit the matching keys on the beat. OCTIVE is a free vertical scrolling rhythm game (VSRG) for 1 to 20 keys, on Windows, Android and in your browser.

Download freePlay in your browser

What you can do

  • Play 4K, 7K and any key count from 1 to 20, with long notes, star ratings and osu!mania-style accuracy.
  • Bring your osu!mania maps: import your whole osu! Songs folder or single .osz and .osu files. See osu!mania maps in OCTIVE.
  • Climb the global performance ranking (PR, like osu!'s pp), play Ranked matches, daily charts and dans.
  • Play multiplayer rooms with friends, chat, and watch friends play live.
  • Share and download charts, note skins, menu themes and whole styles in the Workshop, and import osu! skins.
  • Make your own charts in the chart editor, including Friday Night Funkin' songs and modcharts.

Free, and on every device

OCTIVE is free to play. Get the Windows installer or the Android app on the download page, or play in your browser with nothing to install. An account is optional: it puts your scores on the global ranking and keeps them in sync between devices.

For VSRG and mania players

If you play osu!mania, Etterna, Quaver or other mania-style rhythm games, OCTIVE will feel familiar: scroll speed, judgement windows, mods (Hidden, Hard Rock, Double Time, Mirror and more), a calibration for audio offset, and a star rating that counts speed, chords and rhythm changes.

Questions

Is OCTIVE free?

Yes. OCTIVE is free on Windows, Android and in the browser. An optional supporter purchase only adds cosmetics and never affects scores.

What is a VSRG?

A vertical scrolling rhythm game: notes fall down lanes toward a judgement line and you press the key for each lane in time with the music, like osu!mania, Etterna or Quaver. OCTIVE is one.

Which key counts does OCTIVE support?

1 to 20 keys. Most charts are 4K and 7K, and there are 5K, 6K, 8K and more in the Workshop.

Can I play osu!mania maps?

Yes. Import your osu! Songs folder or single .osz / .osu files. osu!standard, taiko and catch maps can be converted to 4K too.

Can I play with friends?

Yes: multiplayer rooms, private rooms for two, a global chat and live spectating for friends.
