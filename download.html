Questions

Is OCTIVE safe to install?

Yes. It's made by one developer, and the installer and app come straight from OCTIVE's own server. Windows may warn about new installers: choose More info, then Run anyway. OCTIVE is also on the Microsoft Store, which installs it without that warning.

Does OCTIVE cost anything?

No, it's free. Supporter is an optional purchase for cosmetics only.
