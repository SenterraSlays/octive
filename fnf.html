Friday Night Funkin' charts in OCTIVE

Turn FNF mods into OCTIVE charts. OCTIVE's chart editor reads Friday Night Funkin' songs from the popular engines and plays them as 4K charts, with their modcharts.

Download freePlay in your browser

Importing an FNF song

In the chart editor, pick the song's instrumental, vocals and chart: each one is optional and the BPM is filled in for you. Psych Engine (0.x and 1.0), Kade, V-Slice and Codename mods all work.

Psych Engine modcharts (Lua and HScript) are found and played with the chart, and events next to the chart are read too.

Then play it like any chart

Your FNF chart gets a star rating and PR, works with mods and multiplayer, and can be shared in the Workshop (the "fnf" tag) for others to play.
