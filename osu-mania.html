Play your osu!mania maps in OCTIVE

OCTIVE is a free VSRG that reads osu!mania beatmaps directly. Bring your whole osu! library over in one click and keep playing your favourite maps with a new ranking, Workshop and modes.

Download freePlay in your browser

Importing your maps

  • On Windows, open Song Select and use the folder button to import your whole osu! Songs folder.
  • Or import single .osz and .osu files, on any device.
  • osu!standard, taiko and catch maps can be converted to 4K (Settings → osu! songs).
  • Import osu! skins (.osk) in Settings → Style, converted as well as possible.

Scoring like osu!mania

Accuracy uses osu!mania's weights (Marvelous 320, Perfect 300, Great 200, Good 100, Bad 50). Every chart gets a star rating, and your plays earn PR, like osu!'s performance points, and your total adds up your best play on every chart at its full value.

Mods work as you'd expect: Easy, Hard Rock, Hidden, Fade In, Half Time, Double Time (with or without pitch shift) and Mirror.

What OCTIVE adds

  • A global PR ranking, Ranked matchmaking, daily charts and dans.
  • A Workshop to share charts, skins, themes and styles.
  • Multiplayer rooms with friends, and a browser version you can play anywhere.

Questions

Does OCTIVE work with osu! maps?

Yes. It imports osu!mania .osz and .osu files and whole osu! Songs folders, and can convert other osu! modes to 4K.

Do I need osu! installed?

No. You can import .osz files you downloaded. On Windows, OCTIVE can also link your existing osu! Songs folder.

Is PR the same as osu! pp?

It works the same way: chart difficulty (stars) times accuracy, and every chart's best play adds its full PR to your total. The numbers aren't the same as osu!'s.
