Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information OCTIVE collects, why, who it is shared with and how you can have it removed. It covers the OCTIVE game (Windows and Android), this website (senterraslays.github.io/octive), the OCTIVE server that the game and website talk to, and OCTIVE's Discord integration. In this policy, "OCTIVE", "we" and "us" mean the developer of OCTIVE.

In short: you can play OCTIVE without an account. If you make one, your username, profile and scores are stored with Unity Gaming Services so they work on every device and appear on the public ranking. An email address is optional and only used to send you codes. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies, and we never sell your information.

1. Information we collect

Without an account

  • Data on your device. Your settings, songs, charts, skins, scores and replays are stored on your device. We do not receive them.
  • Update checks. The game asks our server whether a newer version exists and downloads updates from it. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to our hosting provider (Cloudflare), which may keep it in short-lived logs. We don't store it.

With an OCTIVE account

  • Account details: your username, a player ID, and your password. Your password is handled by Unity Authentication; it is stored by Unity in a protected form and we never see it stored. When you sign in on the website, your password is passed to Unity over an encrypted connection through our server and is not saved.
  • Profile: your display name, profile picture, bio, level, experience, performance points, accuracy, total score, play count, full combos, best combo, play time, global rank, badges and the game version you last played. Content creators can also add links to their social media pages.
  • Scores: your best results per chart (score, accuracy, judgements and related details), so your ranking and stats follow you between devices.
  • Online status: the time you were last active and what you're on (desktop, mobile, the browser version, or viewing the website while signed in), used to show friends and the ranking that you're online, for example "Online Desktop" or "Viewing Website". You can hide it with Appear offline in Settings, which also hides website visits.
  • Following: the players you follow and the number of followers you have. Every account follows the OCTIVE developer.
  • Supporter purchases: if you buy supporter time (or someone gifts it to you), we keep the player it's for, who bought it, how many months, the amount, currency and date, and Stripe's reference for the payment. Your supporter looks (coloured name, effect, banner and animated picture) are stored and shown publicly. Your payment details (card, billing name and address, email for the receipt) go to Stripe, not to us.
  • Friends and multiplayer: your friends list, friend requests and messages, and the rooms you create or join (room name, players and the chart being played).
  • Moderation records: if a developer or moderator takes action on an account (for example a ban, a forced name change or a badge), that action is recorded.
  • Email address (optional): only if you link one on the website. We use it only to send verification and password-reset codes. We store it together with the time codes were sent (to limit how many can be sent) and a scrambled form of any code that hasn't been used yet.

On the website

  • When you sign in, your browser keeps sign-in tokens in its local storage so you stay signed in. Signing out removes them.
  • The website does not use cookies, advertising or analytics.

Discord

  • Discord status (Rich Presence). On Windows, while the Discord app is running, the game tells Discord what you're doing (for example "Playing song title [difficulty]" and how long for), so it can be shown on your Discord profile. This goes straight from the game to the Discord app on your computer; we don't receive any information from Discord. You can turn it off in Settings → Profile & data → Discord status. Discord's handling of this information is covered by Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • The OCTIVE Discord application does not read your messages, servers or Discord account details.

2. What is public

If you have an account and have played while signed in, other players and website visitors can see your display name, profile picture, bio, badges, social links (content creators), level, performance points, accuracy, rank, other stats on your profile card, and whether you're online. Your username, password and email address are never shown to anyone.

3. How we use information

  • To run your account, save your progress and sync it between devices.
  • To show the global ranking, player profiles, friends and multiplayer.
  • To send codes you ask for (linking an email, resetting a password).
  • To keep the game fair and safe: preventing cheating, enforcing the Terms of Service and responding to reports.
  • To deliver updates.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers below.

4. Service providers

OCTIVE relies on these companies to work. Each only receives what it needs to provide its service:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: accounts, cloud saves, leaderboards, friends, lobbies and multiplayer relay). Privacy policy
  • Stripe (processes supporter payments). Privacy policy
  • Cloudflare (our server and storage for updates and linked email addresses). Privacy policy
  • GitHub (hosts this website). Privacy statement
  • Brevo (sends verification and password-reset emails). Privacy policy
  • Discord (only if you use Discord status). Privacy policy

These providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States.

5. How long we keep information

Account information is kept while your account exists. A linked email is kept until you remove it or delete your account. Unused codes expire after 15 minutes. Moderation records may be kept after an account is deleted where needed to prevent abuse (for example, to keep a ban in place). Records of supporter purchases are kept for as long as tax and accounting rules require, even after an account is deleted. A website visit only keeps the time of your latest visit.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Change your profile (name, picture, bio, socials) in the game or on the website's Account page.
  • Remove your email at any time on the Account page.
  • Hide your online status with Appear offline, and turn off Discord status, in Settings.
  • Delete your account and its data, or ask for a copy of it, by contacting us (below). We'll confirm the request comes from the account owner, then delete the account, its scores, profile and linked email.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your information. Contact us to use them.

7. Children

OCTIVE accounts are not meant for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you are under that age, please play without an account. If you believe a child has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Connections to our server and to Unity are encrypted, passwords are handled only by Unity, and codes are stored only in scrambled form. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For important changes we will also announce it in the game or in the OCTIVE Discord server.

10. Contact

Questions, data requests or account deletion: open an issue at github.com/SenterraSlays/octive/issues (don't post private details there; we'll reply with a private way to continue), or message a developer in the OCTIVE Discord server.
